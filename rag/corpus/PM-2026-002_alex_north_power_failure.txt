POST-MORTEM REPORT: PM-2026-002
Date: 2026-06-02
Incident: Alex-Fiber-North Power Failure
Affected Node: Node 12 (Alex-Fiber-North)

Timeline (UTC):
14:00 - Commercial power grid failure in the Alexandria North district.
14:00 - UPS system at Node 12 took over the load.
14:05 - UPS battery bank failed prematurely due to undetected degraded cells.
14:05 - Backup diesel generator failed to auto-start due to a faulty Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) controller.
14:06 - Complete loss of power at Node 12. All equipment offline.
15:30 - Facilities engineer arrived on-site.
16:15 - Generator started manually. Power restored to site. Equipment boot sequence initiated.
20:00 - Commercial power restored. UPS running in bypass mode. Services fully stable.

Root Cause:
A cascading failure event. First, the commercial grid failed. The UPS system, which was scheduled for maintenance, failed under load in 5 minutes instead of the rated 4 hours. Compounding this, the backup generator's ATS logic board was faulty and failed to detect the dead bus, thus not triggering the auto-start sequence.

Resolution:
The generator was manually started to restore services. The faulty UPS battery bank and the ATS controller have been flagged for immediate replacement.

Impact:
Total outage duration was approximately 6 hours. Multiple enterprise customers in the Alexandria North region experienced complete service disruption. 

Lessons Learned:
1. UPS battery impedance testing must be conducted quarterly rather than annually.
2. Generator ATS auto-start functionality must be tested under load during monthly maintenance windows.

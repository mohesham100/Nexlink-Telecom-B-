MAINTENANCE VISIT: MV-2026-014
Date: 2026-05-14
Location: Cairo Metro Line 3 Construction Site (near Node 10)
Engineer: Tarek Mahmoud

Objective:
Perform emergency fiber splicing to repair the severed 144-strand trunk cable caused by excavation equipment.

Work Performed:
1. Dispatched on an emergency basis, arriving on-site at 09:00 UTC.
2. Coordinated with construction crew to secure the area and halt digging.
3. Located the severed ends of the armored fiber cable in the trench.
4. Pulled sufficient slack from the adjacent handholes to allow for a splice closure.
5. Prepped both ends of the cable: stripped the armor, buffer tubes, and cleaned the fibers.
6. Utilized a Fujikura core-alignment fusion splicer to splice all 144 strands.
7. Installed splice protectors and organized the fibers within a waterproof splice enclosure (FOSC).
8. Sealed the enclosure and secured it in the handhole.
9. Coordinated with the NOC to perform OTDR testing from Node 10 to verify the integrity of the splices.

Results:
The splicing was completed successfully. OTDR measurements indicated an average splice loss of 0.15 dB per strand, which is well within the acceptable specification of <0.3 dB. The overall link budget was restored to nominal levels, and the NOC confirmed all services were back online.

Notes:
This is a temporary emergency repair. A permanent maintenance window must be scheduled to replace the damaged section of the cable entirely and potentially reroute it away from the active construction zone to prevent recurrence.

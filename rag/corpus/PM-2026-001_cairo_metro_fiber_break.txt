POST-MORTEM REPORT: PM-2026-001
Date: 2026-05-14
Incident: Cairo Metro Line 3 Excavation Fiber Break
Affected Node: Node 10 (Alex-Fiber-Main)

Timeline (UTC):
08:14 - Critical alarms triggered for Node 10 backbone link. Loss of signal detected.
08:15 - NOC escalated incident. Bank of Alexandria VIP circuit down.
08:30 - Field team dispatched to suspected break location near Cairo Metro Line 3 extension.
09:00 - Field team arrived. Confirmed massive fiber trunk severed by heavy excavation machinery.
09:12 - Emergency fusion splicing initiated.
09:55 - Final splice completed. Optical power restored.
10:01 - Services fully restored and verified.

Root Cause:
A contractor for the Cairo Metro Line 3 project was excavating outside their permitted zone and struck the primary subterranean fiber conduit, completely severing the 144-strand trunk that serves Node 10. 

Resolution:
An emergency splice was performed on the severed cables. The splice restored connectivity, although we will need to schedule a maintenance window to lay a permanent replacement section of the cable to ensure long-term stability.

Impact:
The total downtime was 107 minutes for general traffic. Specifically, the Bank of Alexandria VIP circuit experienced a 47-minute hard down state before partial traffic rerouting took effect. The SLA for VIP customers dictates <15 minutes response, which was met, but the overall downtime breached the 99.999% uptime guarantee.

Lessons Learned:
1. Improved physical route diversity is required for Node 10. A secondary path should be established to prevent single-point-of-failure from construction.
2. Closer coordination with Cairo municipal construction authorities is needed to map our conduits.

STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: PROC-SLA-STD
Subject: Standard Tier Customer Escalation Protocol

Purpose:
To outline the handling procedures for Standard-tier customers (e.g., Delta Logistics, residential broadband aggregators) experiencing downtime.

Procedure:
1. Detection: Alarms are logged into the ticketing system and handled in a First-In, First-Out (FIFO) manner, unless a widespread regional outage is detected.
2. Response Time: The NOC must acknowledge the ticket within <4 hours during business hours.
3. Resolution: Service restoration is handled on a "best-effort" basis. There are no strict Time To Repair (TTR) guarantees, though the internal target is next-business-day resolution.
4. Engineering Dispatch: Field dispatches are scheduled based on engineer availability, typically within 24-48 hours.
5. Communication: Communication is primarily handled via automated ticket updates.

Standard SLA customers do not receive dedicated account managers or mandatory post-incident reports. However, systemic issues affecting multiple standard users should be escalated internally to prevent broader network degradation.

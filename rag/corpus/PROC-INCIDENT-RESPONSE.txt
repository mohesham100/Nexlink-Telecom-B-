STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: PROC-INCIDENT-RESPONSE
Subject: Incident Response Protocol

Purpose:
To standardize the response to unscheduled network events and outages.

Procedure:
1. Triage & Classification: Upon receiving an alarm, the NOC must classify the severity:
   - P1 (Critical): Total node outage, VIP customer down, or major security breach.
   - P2 (High): Enterprise customer down, severe degradation, redundancy lost.
   - P3 (Medium): Single standard customer down, minor degradation.
   - P4 (Low): Informational alarms, cosmetic issues.
2. Assign Owner: Every incident must have a single designated Incident Commander (usually the senior NOC engineer on shift).
3. Communication: Establish a bridge call or dedicated Slack channel for P1/P2 incidents. Regular updates must be pushed to stakeholders.
4. Resolution: Focus entirely on service restoration. Root cause analysis can follow later; mitigating the immediate impact is the priority.
5. Post-Mortem: A PIR must be conducted for all P1 and P2 incidents to identify the root cause and implement preventative measures.
